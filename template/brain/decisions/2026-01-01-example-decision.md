# Example: change orders after sign-off go back through the estimator

Date: 2026-01-01
Decided by: (name/role)
Decision: After a proposal is signed, any scope change is re-estimated by the estimator before the PM confirms it with the client.
Why: Two jobs in 2025 lost margin because PMs approved changes verbally. The estimator owns price; the PM owns the relationship.
Revisit when: the PM role gets its own pricing authority, or after 10 change orders under this rule.
